Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

FoodIQ ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

We collect only the information needed to provide the app's core features:

  • Profile data you choose to enter: name, age (optional), allergens, dietary preferences, family member profiles. Stored locally on your device.
  • Scan history: barcodes, product names, and timestamps of products you scan. Stored locally on your device. You can clear it at any time from Settings.
  • Subscription status (Free or Pro), managed through Apple billing — we never see your payment details.
  • Anonymous usage analytics (which screens you visit, errors that occur). No personal identifiers are sent.

2. What we do NOT collect

  • We don't collect photos, names of friends or contacts.
  • We don't sell or share your data with advertisers.
  • We don't track you across other apps or websites.

3. Third-party services

The app communicates with the following services to fetch product data and process subscriptions:

  • Open Food Facts — to look up product details by barcode (public open database).
  • RevenueCat — for subscription management.
  • Sentry — for crash and error reporting (anonymous).

4. Your rights

You can review, modify, or delete your profile and scan history at any time directly inside the app. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data.

5. Children

FoodIQ is intended for general audiences and not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Material changes will be communicated in-app.

7. Contact

For questions, write to app.support.535@gmail.com.
